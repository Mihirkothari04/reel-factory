# Mode: Rapid Catalog (`catalog`)

**Identity:** maximum value per second. A preview of everything up front (open loop), items fly past with a counter and progress, and the reel ends on a dense cheat sheet built to be screenshotted and shared. (Creator 63's 30 plugins; Creator 62' 45 places; Creator 48's 20 repos; Creator 20 micro-lists; batch-02's launch and legal checklists.)

## Three densities
| variant | items | per item | length |
|---|---|---|---|
| deep list | 3–7 | 5–8 s, one demo each (`item` + a demo scene) | 40–60 s |
| fast catalog | 10–30 | 1–2 s (`carousel`, 3–6 cards per scene) | 35–60 s |
| micro-list | 4–6 | ~1 s, names only; value in the caption/DM | 12–20 s (loops) |

## Construction
- **Hook:** the number + promise + a preview: a `grid` of all items, ToC chips (`meta.progress = {"style": "chips", "items": […], "scenes": […]}`: all chips appear at once, each fills when its section starts), or the challenge "I bet you know fewer than 10".
- **Order:** by job ("only look at your row") or escalating wow (strongest second-to-last).
- **Counter:** `carousel` shows `06 / 30` (`total`, `offset`).
- **Concept cards (checklists):** one illustrated icon card per item in a locked style (e.g. "isometric 3D icon, soft studio light, white background, single blue accent, no text") — the legal-checklist pattern.
- **Restraint beat:** "Don't install all 30. Pick one row." (credibility).
- **End frame:** `sheet` with every item (two columns past 8), `note` "checked 12 Nov 2026", `stamp` "Screenshot this"; hold ≥ 2.5 s; set `cover_at` there.
- **CTA:** save/share ("send it to a friend who's about to launch") or a withheld bonus ("I'll DM all 30 plus 5 more").
- **Theme:** `swiss` or `night`; captions `highlight`.

## Pitfalls
- Reading 30 descriptions aloud: the VO names the item and its job; details live on the cards and the sheet.
- Invented star counts: only live numbers from `RF sources repo`.
- A title that promises something the list doesn't deliver (batch-02 "lifetime free AI tools" over animation libraries): the hook must match the items.
