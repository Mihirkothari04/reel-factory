# Category `oss-alternative`: a free / open-source thing against a paid or famous incumbent

**Read only when** the reel positions a free/open-source tool *against* something the viewer pays for or already knows (Cursor, Perplexity, Google, Electron, LangChain, paid AI subscriptions). Evidence: 6 batch-04 reels (median 45 s, 35–49; 214 wpm) plus earlier corpus reels of the same shape (Creator 37 "5 free repos replace $1,000+/mo", Creator 22 Wan2GP "no $3,000 GPU"). Usually combined with `github-repo`, sometimes with `top-n`.

## Who watches and why the reel is built this way
The viewer feels a cost (a subscription, a limit, a slow app). The reel's tension is **villain → replacement**: it starts with the thing they already know, so the first second needs no explanation, and the free alternative arrives as relief. That is why these reels open on the incumbent's logo or price, not on the new tool.

## Beat order (villain first, 35–55 s)
| beat | time | content |
|---|---|---|
| Villain | 0–4 s | the incumbent named and shown: its official logo animating on its name (brand roll call), its real pricing page or limitation screen; "Forget X and Y" / "Stop paying for X, Y or Z" / the hidden cost ("4 GB of RAM every morning") |
| Strike + replacement | 2–9 s | the rival logo struck out (gfx `sticker` + `strike`), the alternative named with scale (stars) and authority (builder, company) |
| Explain the villain (optional) | | why the incumbent hurts, with its *own* primary source (Electron's docs highlighted) |
| Parity | | 2–3 features matched to what the incumbent sells, each **shown working** |
| Differentiator / reframe | ~50 % | the thing the incumbent can't do (local, self-hosted, no account) or "they didn't build a wrapper, they built…" |
| Price anchor | | itemised against the features, or the subscriptions summed and struck ($200 + $99 + $249… → "$1,000+/mo"); real pricing screenshots only |
| Honest gap | | what you give up (setup, polish, your own API key, "log in with accounts you already pay for") |
| So-what + CTA | last 6–8 s | optional "if you…" triad (saver / builder / do-nothing → falling behind), then the keyword |

## Two forms
- **Price-gap form** (paid incumbent): price anchor is the spine; "free" lands early.
- **No-price-gap form** (the rivals are free too, e.g. LangChain/CrewAI): lead with "better" (scale, authority, architecture), feature parity, and keep "100 % open source and free" for the last re-hook.

## Construction with our studio engine
- `presenter` split: incumbent logos in the top band above the presenter on the names, struck on the beat; the presenter delivers the turn ("…and it's free").
- Real captures: the rival's pricing/limitation page and the alternative's repo/site (`RF shot`); testimonials or community outputs as real screenshots only.
- gfx: `sticker` logos (+ `strike`), `counter` for the summed price, `label` + `icon` pills for each matched feature.

## Pitfalls seen in this category
- **Unsourced stats** in AMPLIFY ("68,000 developers just deleted Cursor") and **false recency** ("shipped last week" for a years-old project).
- **Unmeasured multipliers** ("20x LIGHTER") as posters with no benchmark.
- Calling it free when it needs a paid key or login: say so (honest gap).
- Real people as decoration (a head-of-state photo for "China"); attacking a person rather than a product. Name the company/product, never mock a person.
- Parity asserted, not shown.

Evidence (batch-04): Creator 97 (OpenHands vs Cursor; Puter vs Google/Microsoft/Perplexity), Creator 86 (AgentScope vs LangChain/CrewAI: logo strike), Creator 92 (Pake vs Electron: villain explained with its own docs), Creator 77 (5 free repos "that should be illegal": verbal price anchors), Creator 37 (5 free repos: struck subscription receipt), Creator 70 (Pake: the floor, a 12 s slide reel).
